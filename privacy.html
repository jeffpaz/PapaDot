Privacy Policy for PapaDot

Last updated: January 4, 2026

PapaDot ("the App") is a golf scoring and betting tracker developed by Jeff Pazahanick.

Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the App.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

  • All golf scores, bets, player names, and game data are stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not require account registration, login, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics services, third-party tracking, or advertising that collects data.

In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases (e.g., to remove ads or unlock features). These are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Device Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive device features.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact Us
