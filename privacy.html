Privacy Policy for PapaDot

Last updated: January 04, 2026

PapaDot ("the App") is a golf scoring and betting tracker developed by Jeff Pazahanick.

Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the App.

Information We Collect

The App collects limited personal information only as necessary for its functionality. This may include:

  • Golf scores, bets, player names, and game data, which are stored locally on your device.
  • Access to your contacts (with permission) to easily add players to games.
  • Access to your location (with permission) to suggest nearby golf courses or log game locations.
  • Access to your camera (with permission) to take photos of scorecards or the course for reference within the App.

We do not require account registration or login. We do not use analytics services, third-party tracking, or advertising that collects data beyond what's needed for core features. Any data accessed via permissions is used solely for App functionality and is not shared with third parties unless required by law.

In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases (e.g., to remove ads or unlock features). These are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Device Permissions

The App requests access to the following device features for enhanced functionality:

  • Camera: To allow you to capture photos of scorecards, the golf course, or other relevant images directly within the App.
  • Location: To provide location-based services, such as recommending nearby golf courses or associating games with specific locations.
  • Contacts: To enable quick selection of players from your contact list for multiplayer games.

These permissions are optional and requested only when needed. You can manage them in your device settings. We do not access your microphone, photos (beyond camera use), or other sensitive features unless explicitly stated.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
