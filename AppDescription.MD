Why PapaDot?
Built purely for fun rounds with friends — not serious handicap systems. Track exciting challenges like fairways, birdies, poleys, greenies, and low holes with automatic points, live leaderboards, and easy math. Set stakes, tap scores, and enjoy the game.
Key Features
Dynamic Carry-Over System
Signature feature: Missed greenies on par 3s roll over and grow in value (2 → 4 → 6+ dots). Same system for Low Hole — ties build the pot until someone wins outright.
Automatic Low Hole
No arguments. PapaDot calculates net scores using handicaps and awards the hole automatically. Ties carry over.
Team Mode (2v2)
Perfect for foursomes. Dots go to teams and split between partners. Penalties hit the opposing team. Ideal for best-ball wagers.
Handicap-Adjusted Scoring
Enter handicaps (0-36). PapaDot applies strokes per hole. Special par 3 rule: 20+ handicaps get max 1 stroke; under 20 get none — keeps it fair.
Live Home Screen Widgets
View live standings at a glance. Auto-updates every 30 seconds. Small, Medium, Large sizes. Tap to open scorecard.
Real-Time Multiplayer
Share simple join codes. Live sync via iCloud across devices. Offline fallback + smart conflict prevention.
Smart Course Integration
Search 40,000+ GPS courses. Auto-detects par 3s with real par, yardage, and handicaps. Manual entry available.
Customizable Rules
Set point values, save presets, add custom tasks, adjust stakes, and choose starting hole.
What Gets Tracked
Positive: Fairway Hit, Birdie, Poley, Greenie (carry-over), Low Hole (carry-over), Sandy.
Penalties: Sand, OB, 3-Putt, 4-Putt (-3), Lady’s Tee (-3).
Smart Birdie Toggle — Automatically adjusts score to one under par.
Beautiful, Fast Interface
Quick-tap scoring with haptics, real-time dots counter, inline par/yardage, special Par 3 views. Supports 2-6 players (4 for teams).
Game History & Payments
Review past rounds by course, track trends, share via iMessage. Instant settlement shows exactly who owes whom with simple pairwise calculations.
Perfect For
Casual golfers, weekend warriors, regular foursomes, and anyone who wants friendly competition and easy settling up — without the complexity of traditional golf apps.
